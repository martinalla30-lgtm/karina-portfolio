About

I'm Karina, and I photograph weddings because few days hold so much feeling in so few hours. What stays with me is rarely the staged moment. It's the deep breath before the doors open, the friend who can't stop laughing, the look between two people who have briefly forgotten anyone else is in the room.

On the day itself, I stay close enough to see everything and quiet enough to be forgotten. I guide you only when it helps, and otherwise let the day unfold as it will. What you receive afterwards is a story told in black and white, honest and unhurried, and as true in fifty years as it is today.
